Notes

  • The wave pins to the centre at both ends and tucks behind the dot and stem, so the three parts read as one letter.
  • Loader: the stem fills as the page loads while the wave "talks"; at 100% the dot lights up and the wave settles.
  • All shapes are solid. Only the wave is a stroke, so outline it in Figma before using it in Canva or print.
